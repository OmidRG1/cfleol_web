Who We Are

Qui sommes-nous

The Canadian Forest Landscape Earth Observation Lab (CFLEOL) is a remote sensing research group based at the Laurentian Forestry Centre, Canadian Forest Service, Natural Resources Canada, in Québec City.

We develop and deliver national-scale earth observation data products for Canada's forested ecosystems — covering forest land cover, structural attributes, and stand-replacing disturbances including wildfire, harvest, insect outbreaks, and windthrow.

Our work spans the full Canadian land surface — from dense boreal forests to open wetlands, bryoid areas, lichen fields, and rocky substrates — providing consistent, wall-to-wall national baselines for science, policy, and carbon reporting.

Le Laboratoire canadien d'observation de la Terre des paysages forestiers développe des produits de données d'observation de la Terre à l'échelle nationale pour les écosystèmes forestiers canadiens.

National Forest Information System
Système national d'information forestière (SNIF)

The NFIS is Canada's primary portal for national forest information, data products, and monitoring tools. CFLEOL contributes national earth observation products — disturbance maps and forest inventory — to the NFIS open data ecosystem.

Le SNIF est le portail principal du Canada pour les informations forestières nationales. CFLEOL contribue des produits nationaux d'observation de la Terre — cartes de perturbations et inventaire forestier — à l'écosystème de données ouvertes du SNIF.
